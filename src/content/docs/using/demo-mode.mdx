Demo mode turns the keyboard into its own display piece. It plays a loop of about
**15 minutes** and then starts over, until someone **holds Esc for two seconds**.

## Start and stop

The demo needs no computer: it also runs with the keyboard on a USB charger or a power bank.

1. Open the [settings layer](/using/keymaps/#the-utility-and-settings-layers) and tap
   [`More…`](/using/keymaps/#more).
2. Tap **`Demo Mode`** on the row that appears.

To stop, **hold Esc for two seconds**. The keyboard goes back to your base layout and your own
language. A short tap on Esc, or any other key, does nothing, so visitors can press keys without
ending the demo.

The status displays say how to stop it: every 12 seconds they show **Demo mode / Hold Esc to
exit** for 4 seconds. The rest of the time they show the usual layer, language and typing speed.

<Aside type="caution">
While the demo runs, **no key reaches the computer**. Every key press is ignored, so the keyboard
seems dead to anyone who does not know about the Esc hold. That is why the key sits behind
`More…`, where a stray tap does not reach it.
</Aside>

## What the loop shows

Seven blocks of activity, each followed by about a minute of rest:

| Block | What happens on the keycaps |
|---|---|
| Typing | Sentences typed key by key. Each key lights up as it is pressed, and Shift flips every legend to upper case. Fn and Num are held for a few seconds each. |
| Languages | Short words with the keycaps in Greek, Russian, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Georgian and Armenian. |
| Menus | The [language menu](/using/languages/) and the emoji picker page through their tabs, then the Intl, utility and settings layers. |
| Glyph scripts | Tengwar, runes, Cirth, Aurebesh, the Standard Galactic Alphabet, IBM VGA, C64, Amiga and Braille. See [Glyph Scripts](/using/glyph-scripts/). |
| Code | A few lines of C and a `git` command, with brackets and symbols on Shift. |
| European languages | German and French, each followed by the Intl layer with its accented letters, then Ukrainian and the emoji picker. |
| Plain typing | A longer stretch of text, the language menu once more, the settings layer. |

The rest between blocks is your own [idle style](/using/idle/): the keycaps fade over
10 seconds, then the style you picked runs for about a minute. The demo then wakes the
keyboard and the next block starts.

The typing follows **your base layout**. On Colemak or Neo the demo presses the keys that carry
each letter on that layout.

## What it does not change

- **Your language.** The language tour is a preview on the keycaps only. The host app keeps
  seeing your real language, and nothing is saved.
- **Your settings.** Brightness, idle style, glyph script and layout stay as they were.
- **Across a restart.** Demo mode is not saved. Unplug the keyboard and it comes back normal.

A language or glyph script needs its font on the keyboard. Without the [font pack](/firmware/font-packs/)
those steps show your own language instead of blank keys.

<Aside type="note">
If the computer goes to sleep, the demo pauses with the displays and continues where it was when
the computer wakes. A firmware or font update stops the demo.
</Aside>

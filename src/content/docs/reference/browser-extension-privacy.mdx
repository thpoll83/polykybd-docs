This is the privacy policy for the **PolyKybd Website Reporter** browser extension, which
helps [PolyKybdHost](/software/overview/) show [website-specific keycap overlays](/using/website-detection/).
It describes exactly what the extension accesses and where that data goes.

## What it accesses

- The **URL** and **title** of your **currently active browser tab**.
- Whether the browser window is **focused**.

It does **not** read page content, form data, cookies, history, or any other tab. It activates
only on tab switches, navigation, and window-focus changes.

## Where the data goes

- Reports are sent **only to `http://127.0.0.1` (localhost)** — the PolyKybdHost application
  running on the **same computer**.
- **Nothing is transmitted over the internet.** The data is not sent to the developer, to
  PolyKybd / PolyTasten, or to any third party.
- The data is used solely, in the moment, to pick the matching keyboard overlay. The extension
  does not log or retain your browsing activity.

## What it stores

- Only your **settings** — the host port and an optional token — saved with the browser's
  extension storage on your device. No browsing data is stored.

## Data sharing / selling

None. The extension does not sell data and does not share it with anyone.

<Aside type="note">
Under both Chrome and Mozilla policies, active-tab URLs are classified as **web browsing
activity**. The extension collects it strictly to power the website-aware overlay feature and
transmits it only to the local PolyKybdHost app.
</Aside>

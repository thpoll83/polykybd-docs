## The ecosystem

Five open repositories, wired together in [Architecture & Ecosystem](/introduction/architecture/):
[hardware](https://github.com/thpoll83/PolyKybd) (KiCad PCBs, case, stems) ·
[firmware](https://github.com/thpoll83/qmk_firmware) (QMK on RP2040) ·
[PolyKybdHost](https://github.com/thpoll83/PolyKybdHost) (daemon, tray app, `polyctl`) ·
[Adafruit GFX fork](https://github.com/thpoll83/adafruit-gfx-library) (display rendering) ·
[CTND](https://github.com/thpoll83/polykybd-ctnd) (hardware-in-the-loop test rig).

Independent open hardware — say hello on [Discord](https://discord.gg/gW8JescH7M), support it on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/polykb).

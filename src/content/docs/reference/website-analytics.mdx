This documentation site uses **Google Analytics 4** to count visits, so we can see which
pages people actually read and which ones nobody can find. It is **off until you say yes** —
nothing is stored in your browser unless you accept the small 🍪 prompt in the corner.

This page describes exactly what that collects, and lets you change your mind whenever
you like.

## Your current setting

<div data-pk-consent-control>
  <p>
    Analytics only runs on the published site, with JavaScript enabled. If you are seeing
    this text, nothing is being collected and there is nothing to change.
  </p>
</div>

Turning it off also deletes the analytics cookies described below. The choice is remembered
per browser (and per device), so you may see the prompt again on another one.

## What it collects

Once you accept, Google Analytics records, for each page you open:

- The **page address and title**, and the **referrer** — the page or search that sent you here.
- An **approximate location** (city level at best), derived from your IP address.
- Your **browser, operating system, device type** and screen size.
- **Timestamps**, so visits can be grouped into sessions.

## What it never collects

- **Nothing you type.** There are no forms on this site, and no keystrokes are recorded.
- **Nothing from your keyboard or from PolyKybdHost.** This is website traffic only —
  the host application's own reporting is a separate thing entirely, described in
  [Usage Data & Privacy](/software/telemetry/).
- **No name, email, or account** — the site has no login.
- **Nothing at all** if you decline, or before you answer.

## Cookies

Accepting lets Google Analytics set two first-party cookies in your browser:

| Cookie | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Distinguishes one visitor from another |
| `_ga_8JB88YY4E5` | Keeps track of the current session |

Both are set by the site you are on, not by a third-party domain, and neither is used for
advertising. Their exact lifetime is set by Google Analytics — your browser's developer
tools will show the current expiry. Turning analytics off using the control above deletes
both.

## Before you answer

Until you accept, the tag runs in Google's **Consent Mode** with storage denied. In that
state it sets no cookies and sends only anonymous, cookieless signals — no identifier that
can follow you between visits. Declining keeps it that way permanently.

<Aside type="note">
Advertising features are switched off outright: `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and
`ad_personalization` are denied and are never granted, whatever you choose here. Accepting
enables `analytics_storage` and nothing else.
</Aside>

## Where the data goes

Collected data is processed by **Google** as the analytics provider, under
[their terms](https://business.safety.google/adsprocessorterms/). It is used only to
understand documentation traffic. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else.
